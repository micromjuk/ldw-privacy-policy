Line Defense Wars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Line Defense Wars is a small, independently-made tower-defense game by Lucas Persson (Sweden). It collects the minimum data needed to run leaderboards, matches and progression — no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

WHAT WE COLLECT

An anonymous player ID, generated automatically. A display name you choose (shown to other players on leaderboards and in matches), and the portrait and portrait frame you pick. Gameplay stats tied to your player ID: your rating, matches played, Daily Challenge scores, and any promo codes you redeem. If you choose to link it, your Apple Game Center or Google Play Games identity — entirely optional.

WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT

No email address, phone number or real name is required to play. No location data. No access to your contacts, photos or microphone. No advertising or analytics SDKs. No device identifiers are logged by our own code.

WHY WE PROCESS IT, AND WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

To run matchmaking, leaderboards and your progression between sessions. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. It's processed by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services) as our infrastructure provider, and by Apple through Game Center or Google through Google Play Games, only if you choose to link your account.

WHERE IT'S PROCESSED, AND HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Unity's, Apple's and Google's servers may be located outside the European Economic Area, including the United States, under their own standard safeguards for international transfers. We keep your data for as long as your player profile exists.

YOUR RIGHTS

If you're in the EU, UK or a place with similar data protection laws, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or object to how your data is processed. You can delete your data yourself at any time from the game's Settings menu, using the DELETE MY DATA button. This permanently deletes your player account and what's stored with it: your player ID, display name, selected portrait and frame, rating, matches played, unlocked portraits, promo-code redemptions and Game Center or Google Play Games link, plus the progress saved on your device. Your entries on the live leaderboards are deleted, and so are the records of your multiplayer matches and any name reports you made or that were made about you. Results from earlier days of the Daily Challenge may remain in archived leaderboards that we cannot edit or delete, and they show the display name you used at the time. Technical logs kept by our service provider may contain your internal player ID for a limited time. This cannot be undone. For anything else, email ldw.linedefencewars@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown at the top of the in-game Privacy Policy screen) so we can find your data — or your in-game display name and roughly when you played if you no longer have access to it. We aim to respond within 30 days.

OTHER THINGS TO KNOW

Your display name is public — please don't use your real name if you don't want it visible to other players. This game is not directed at children; if you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we'll delete it. This policy may change.
